You often need only a few pages from a long PDF — a chapter, an invoice section, or pages for a form. Many “split PDF” websites ask you to upload the whole document. If the file is sensitive, that is a risk you do not have to take.

## When to split a PDF

- Send only the pages someone requested
- Separate a scanned packet into smaller files
- Pull a range for printing
- Keep the original intact while sharing a subset

## Local split vs upload converters

Upload-based tools send your file to a remote server, process it, then let you download the result. That is convenient, but your document leaves your device.

QuickFile Tools runs the split in your browser. Choose a page range, extract, and download — without creating an account.

## Steps

1. Open the [PDF Split](/tools/pdf-split) tool.
2. Choose your PDF. The tool shows how many pages it contains.
3. Set **From page** and **To page**.
4. Click **Extract pages** and download the new PDF.

Need the opposite workflow? Use [Merge PDF Online](/tools/merge-pdf-online) to combine files again.

## Tips

- Page numbers are 1-based (page 1 is the first page).
- Encrypted PDFs may fail until you remove protection on a copy you own.
- Huge PDFs can use a lot of browser memory — split into smaller ranges if needed.
