Large images slow down emails, forms, and websites. You do not need a desktop app or a cloud uploader to fix that.

## Why browser-based compression helps

When compression runs in your browser, the image never has to leave your device. That is useful for screenshots, ID photos, and anything you would rather not send to a random server.

## Steps with QuickFile Tools

1. Open the [Image Compress](/tools/image-compress) tool.
2. Choose a JPG, PNG, or WebP file.
3. Adjust quality if you want a smaller file (lower quality = smaller size).
4. Download the compressed result.

## Tips for better results

- Photos usually compress well as JPG.
- Screenshots with text often look sharper as PNG, but may stay larger.
- If the file is still big, try a lower quality setting or resize first with [Image Resize](/tools/image-resize).

## Privacy note

QuickFile Tools processes the selected file locally in your browser session. We do not upload the image contents to our servers.
