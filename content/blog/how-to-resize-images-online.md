Resizing is one of the fastest ways to make photos ready for avatars, listings, or social posts.

## When to resize

- Profile pictures with a required pixel size
- Product photos that must match a template
- Reducing resolution before compressing further

## Steps with QuickFile Tools

1. Open [Image Resize](/tools/image-resize).
2. Select your image.
3. Enter width and height. Keep **Lock aspect ratio** on unless you intentionally want to stretch the image.
4. Click **Resize**, then download.

## Combine with compression

After you have the right dimensions, use [Image Compress](/tools/image-compress) to reduce file size for sharing.

## Privacy note

Resizing happens on your device. Your file is not uploaded to QuickFile Tools servers.
